# Company Definition — DaimonUpsidown Education

## 1. Company Overview

**DaimonUpsidown Education** is a fictional Japan-based ALT and English-teacher dispatch company created for this Data Analytics project.

The company recruits foreign teachers and dispatches them to schools, educational organizations, and businesses that provide English-language environments.

The company operates primarily across:

* Tokyo
* Saitama
* Chiba
* Kanagawa

All data used in this project is synthetic and created for educational and portfolio purposes.

---

## 2. Business Model

DaimonUpsidown Education provides a complete teacher recruitment, training, dispatch, support, and retention service.

The company is responsible for:

* Recruiting foreign teachers
* Interviewing candidates
* Hiring teachers
* Supporting work/visa-related administration
* Onboarding employees
* Providing initial training
* Providing on-the-job training
* Providing ongoing training
* Dispatching teachers to clients
* Monitoring teacher performance
* Supporting teachers
* Collecting employee feedback
* Collecting client feedback
* Managing client complaints
* Supporting client relationships
* Reassigning teachers when necessary
* Managing employee retention
* Managing turnover
* Conducting offboarding and exit interviews
* Managing rehire eligibility

The company's overall objective is to provide teachers who perform well, are supported by the company, are valued by their schools/clients, and remain with the company for as long as possible.

# 11. Initial Onboarding

The first week focuses on company onboarding and initial preparation.

Onboarding includes:

* Company orientation
* Company policies and administrative procedures
* School/client expectations
* Japanese workplace culture
* Teaching methodology
* Classroom management
* Safety procedures
* Visa/work-support orientation
* Payroll and benefits explanation
* Introduction to support staff

Required documentation is considered part of the company's administrative and policy process rather than a separate onboarding activity.

---

# 12. Initial Training

Initial training occurs during the first week.

The purpose is to prepare teachers for the working environment and establish a baseline of knowledge and skills.

Training covers areas such as:

* Teaching methodology
* Classroom management
* School/client expectations
* Japanese workplace culture
* Company procedures
* Safety
* Administrative procedures

---

# 13. On-the-Job Training

During the second week, the newly hired teacher participates in **on-the-job training at a temporary/random school** with a DaimonUpsidown trainer.

The purpose is to observe the teacher in a real classroom environment.

The trainer identifies:

* Strengths
* Development areas
* Training needs
* Classroom performance
* Readiness for the working environment

There is **no pass/fail hiring decision**.

Because DaimonUpsidown operates with continuous demand for teachers, all teachers proceed to their permanent assignment.

The evaluation establishes a **starting performance baseline** for future development and analysis.

---

# 14. Permanent Assignment

After the initial two-week preparation period, the teacher receives a permanent school/client assignment.

The company attempts to assign teachers near their home area when practical.

Assignment decisions consider:

* Teacher home area
* Commute time
* Teacher qualifications
* Teaching experience
* Training results
* School/client requirements
* School location
* Client preferences
* Teacher availability
* Position requirements

Clients can communicate preferences and operational needs, but they do not freely select individual teachers.

DaimonUpsidown makes the final assignment decision.

The company trains teachers to a standard that should allow them to perform across the supported assignment environments.

---

# 15. Assignment & Dispatch

DaimonUpsidown operates with continuous demand for teachers.

Newly hired teachers therefore normally receive an assignment rather than waiting for an available position.

Teachers can remain at the same school for many years when the relationship is successful.

Assignment changes can occur because of:

* Client dissatisfaction
* Employee dissatisfaction
* Teacher request
* Contract ended
* School staffing changes
* Business operational needs
* Performance concerns
* Location/commute considerations
* Other

Assignment turnover and employee turnover are tracked separately.

### Assignment Turnover

A teacher leaves their current school/client assignment but remains employed by DaimonUpsidown.

### Employee Turnover

A teacher leaves DaimonUpsidown entirely.

---

# 17. Client Satisfaction

Client/school satisfaction is measured using a:

| Score | Meaning           |

|     1 | Very Dissatisfied |
|     2 | Dissatisfied      |

# 18. Monthly Client Evaluation

Clients provide feedback **monthly**.

The monthly evaluation includes ratings for:

1. Teaching quality
2. Lesson preparation
3. Classroom management
4. Communication
5. Reliability
6. Attendance
7. Professionalism
8. Relationship with students
9. Relationship with school staff
10. English ability
11. Adaptability

The client survey also includes:

* Overall satisfaction
* Written comments
* Complaint/issue information when applicable
* Assignment continuation/renewal information where appropriate

Numerical ratings and written feedback are kept separate so the analysis can examine both performance measurements and qualitative feedback.

---

# 19. Client Complaints

Clients can report dissatisfaction in two ways:

1. Monthly client survey
2. Directly contacting DaimonUpsidown

Serious problems do not need to wait for the next monthly survey.

The general process is:

```text
Client Dissatisfaction
        ↓
Investigation
        ↓
Support / Coaching / Additional Training
        ↓
Follow-up Review
        ↓
Resolved?
   ┌────┴────┐
  Yes        No
   ↓          ↓
Continue   Reassignment
```

The company attempts to resolve problems before reassignment whenever practical.

---

# 20. Reassignment Approval

When a teacher needs to be reassigned because of a client issue:

```text
Client Dissatisfaction
        ↓
Trainer Investigation
        ↓
Trainer Recommendation
        ↓
Senior Trainer Review
        ↓
Senior Trainer Recommendation
        ↓
Lead Trainer Approval
        ↓
Teacher Reassigned
```

A reassigned teacher may receive a new assignment that is:

* Near their home
* Farther from their home
* In another location

depending on available assignments and business requirements.

---

# 21. General Zoom Training

DaimonUpsidown provides general Zoom training approximately every **2–3 months**.

The purpose is primarily reinforcement and professional development.

Training may include:

* Reviewing previously learned material
* Reinforcing teaching techniques
* Sharing teaching ideas
* Reviewing classroom-management practices
* Addressing common issues
* Continuing professional development

General Zoom training is **not primarily a performance investigation**.

---

# 22. Trainer School Visits

Trainer school visits occur every **6 months**.

The main purposes are:

* Evaluate teacher performance
* Review the teacher's school situation
* Check employee satisfaction
* Identify problems
* Provide limited guidance where needed
* Maintain service quality
* Support client satisfaction

The primary business purpose is to ensure that teachers are performing effectively and that clients are receiving the expected level of service.

# 29. Career Advancement

Career advancement is based on a combination of:

* Time with the company
* Performance
* Qualifications
* Internal evaluations
* Business needs

Strong performance can allow advancement around the 5-year point, while additional development may result in progression closer to 6 years.

Promotion is not based solely on time.

# 36. Contract Structure

Teacher assignments normally operate under **monthly contracts**.

Monthly contracts allow DaimonUpsidown to:

* Address client dissatisfaction
* Provide support
* Correct performance issues
* Reassign teachers when necessary
* Find replacement teachers when needed
* Give clients time to evaluate the relationship

A monthly contract does **not** mean the teacher changes schools every month.

A teacher can remain at the same school for many months or years through repeated monthly renewals.

---

# 37. School-Year Timing

Assignments can begin at any time of year.

However, **April and September** are particularly important periods because they align with common school-year cycles in Japan.

Other start dates are possible depending on business needs and client requirements.

---

# 38. Long-Term Contracts

When a school and teacher develop a successful long-term relationship, the client may request a longer-term/yearly contract.

This creates a potential progression:

```text
Monthly Assignment
       ↓
Client Satisfaction
       ↓
Monthly Renewal
       ↓
Strong Relationship
       ↓
Potential Yearly Contract
```

The company can analyze whether higher client satisfaction is associated with longer-term contracts.

---

# 39. Monthly Contract Renewal

The client ultimately decides whether it wants to continue the teacher's assignment.

DaimonUpsidown also reviews the situation based on:

* Client satisfaction
* Teacher performance
* Employee situation
* Assignment conditions
* Operational needs
* Active complaints or issues

The client and company therefore have separate roles in the renewal process.

---

# 40. Assignment / Contract End Reasons

When an assignment or monthly contract ends, the reason is recorded.

Possible reasons include:

* Client dissatisfaction
* Teacher performance concerns
* Teacher requested transfer
* Operational restructuring
* School no longer needs a teacher
* Teacher availability
* Contract/business changes
* Other

"Contract ended" is therefore not treated as a sufficient explanation by itself.

---

# 41. Employee Retention & Turnover

The project will distinguish between:

### Assignment Retention

The teacher remains at the same school/client assignment.

### Employee Retention

The teacher remains employed by DaimonUpsidown.

### Assignment Turnover

The teacher leaves an assignment but remains employed by DaimonUpsidown.

### Employee Turnover

The teacher leaves DaimonUpsidown entirely.

This distinction is important because changing schools does not necessarily mean that the company lost the employee.

---

Possible reasons include:

* Resignation

* Career change

* Visa/work-status issue

* Dissatisfaction with assignment

# 47. Analytical Principles

The project will follow several important principles.

### Evidence before conclusions

Analytical findings will be based on the data rather than assumptions.

### Correlation is not automatically causation

A relationship between two variables will not automatically be presented as a causal relationship.

### Raw data preservation

Original raw datasets will be preserved separately from cleaned datasets.

### Reproducibility

Cleaning and analysis steps will be documented so that the results can be reproduced.

### Business relevance

Analysis will focus on questions that can help management make better decisions.

### Two-sided perspective

The project will analyze both:

**Employee experience**

and

**Client experience**

because both are important to the success of a teacher-dispatch business.
